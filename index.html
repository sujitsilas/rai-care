Breakthrough MEG Technology

SparkHeal is powered by our patented Magnetoelastic Generator (MEG), the first therapeutic application of this revolutionary platform that has been successfully deployed in cardiovascular monitoring, respiratory tracking, and Parkinson's diagnostics.

The MEG creates bipolar, biphasic electrical impulses that mimic natural physiological signals, delivering biomimetic stimulation that actively promotes tissue regeneration and wound healing.

Magnetomechanical Coupling

Converts physical motion into precise electrical signals with consistent output over thousands of cycles.

Magnetic Induction Layer

Generates therapeutic electrical impulses that resemble EEG and EMG signals for optimal tissue response.

Conductive Hydrogel

Multifunctional interface providing electrode contact, tissue scaffold, and delivery platform for therapeutics.
